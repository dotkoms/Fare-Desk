## How to act on any deal in this file

1. **Re-price at the source.** A deal post is a lead; the airline or operator page is the fact.
2. **Assemble the total.** Bags, seats, booking fee, transfer, and the cost of the dates.
3. **Check the fare rules.** Basic economy and non-refundable fares remove the option value.
4. **Prefer direct booking** for anything you might need to change, and for error fares.
5. **Get the 13-digit ticket number** if it is an error fare, and wait 48–72 hours before booking dependencies.
6. **Do not book non-refundable hotels** until the ticket has survived.
7. **Record the baseline.** A €188 fare is only remarkable if the route's median is €350.
8. **Let it go** if the total or the risk is wrong. There is always another fare.
