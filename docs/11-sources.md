# Sources and caveats

Five research passes plus two follow-up passes, primary documents preferred, secondary sources flagged. Access terms and prices were checked in **October 2026** and will drift.

## What was prioritized

- Vendor pricing pages and developer docs for access claims.
- Regulator, court and EUR-Lex documents for legal claims.
- Peer-reviewed transport economics for pricing behaviour.
- Operator pages for fare rules.
- Deal blogs and API listicles only as leads — several 2026 "best API" pages are affiliate content with inconsistent dates.

## What is uncertain

- **Every price example is illustrative.** "From" fares and worked examples are not live quotes.
- **Split-ticketing savings are company-reported.** The 20–30% figure is a planning assumption, not an audited average.
- **Error-fare honour rates are contested.** Published estimates range from ~70% to ~85%, and most come from parties selling deal alerts.
- **Rail pass prices are dynamic.** Interrail/Eurail figures are indicative; the reported September 2026 merger is supported by Seat61 and the eurail.com redirect, but confirm before buying.
- **Access terms change without notice.** Ryanair rotates client versions; Wizz's search endpoint is bot-gated; DB rate-limits and occasionally blocks.
- **Some 2026 sources are SEO/affiliate content.** They were used only for leads, not for pricing or legal conclusions.
- **This is not legal advice.** Contracts of carriage and local rules still govern your ticket.
- **Live deals are a snapshot.** The file [06-live-deals.md](06-live-deals.md) is dated and will be stale within hours.

Where a claim could not be verified against a primary source, it was left out or marked as uncertain rather than presented as fact.
