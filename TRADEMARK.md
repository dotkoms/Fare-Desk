- Refer to the project by name in articles, reviews, talks and comparisons.
- Link to the project and say your work is “based on Fare Desk” or a “fork of Fare Desk”, if that is accurate.
- Use the name in a package description or README to explain compatibility, provided it is not the primary name of your product.
- Distribute unmodified copies under the Fare Desk name, with this policy included.

## What you may not do

- Use “Fare Desk” or a confusingly similar name as the name of your product, company, service, app, domain, social account or package.
- Use the logo, wordmark or visual identity in a way that suggests endorsement, affiliation or official status.
- Register a domain, trademark, social handle or app-store listing that incorporates the mark.
- Sell merchandise using the mark.
- Remove or alter the trademark notices from distributed copies.

If you fork the code, **rename your fork and remove the Fare Desk name and logo** from the user interface, package names and marketing. You may state that it is derived from Fare Desk.

## Requesting permission

For commercial use of the marks, partnership, or an exception, contact the maintainer through the repository. Permission must be in writing.

## Enforcement

The project owner may revoke permission and take action against uses that confuse users or imply endorsement. This policy is not legal advice; trademark registration and enforcement are separate legal steps.
